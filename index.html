Mechatronics Studio

Deep Systems Course — From classical mechatronics to cyber-physical systems (2026)

Start learning View syllabus

What you will learn

Build a working mental model of mechatronic products — sensing, actuation, embedded control, dynamics, and the modern layers that wrap them.

1Define mechatronics as concurrent multi-domain design, not bolted-on electronics.
2Specify sensors and conditioning with accuracy, dynamics, and noise in mind.
3Choose actuators and understand fluid, mechanical, and electrical options.
4Implement embedded and PLC control patterns with fault awareness.
5Model dynamics, read Bode ideas, and tune PID with anti-windup hygiene.
6Map classical topics to Industry 4.0/5.0, networks, ROS 2, twins, cobots, and OT security.

How the course is structured

Three tracks across eight modules — foundations, depth, then a dedicated modern-systems update.

TRACK A

Foundations

Modules 1–3: what mechatronics is, systems thinking, measurement vs control, sensing, and digital information.

TRACK B

Depth

Modules 4–7: actuation, embedded brains, models and control, design method, and integrated case studies.

TRACK C

Modern systems

Module 8: CPS, smart sensors, FOC drives, industrial Ethernet, ROS 2, digital twins, cobots, and security — the 2026 layer.
