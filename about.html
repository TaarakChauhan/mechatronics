About this course

Mechatronics Studio is a self-contained educational website you can open offline. No installers, no accounts, no server.

How to use the site

  1. Open index.html in any modern browser (double-click or use the macOS Open Course.command helper).
  2. Work lessons in order, or jump from the syllabus dashboard.
  3. Use the left sidebar to navigate; checkmarks appear when you mark a lesson complete.
  4. Submit each lesson quiz for instant feedback; scores are stored locally.
  5. Keyboard: press [ and ] for previous/next lesson when available.
  6. Visit Progress to see completion by module or reset local data.

Learning path

Foundations (Modules 1–3) establish systems vocabulary, sensing, and digital information. Depth (Modules 4–7) cover actuation, embedded/PLC control, modelling, PID, and integrated cases. Modern systems (Module 8) is a dedicated update track: Industry 4.0/5.0, smart sensing, drives, controllers, industrial networks, ROS 2, digital twins, cobots, and OT security. Robot mechanics (Module 9) adds spatial frames, kinematics intuition, Jacobians, discrete control, estimation, planning, and mobile bases. Project Studio (Module 10) is a progressive build track from sensing through closed loops, mobile behaviours, arms, and a ROS 2 simulation stack.

Content note All teaching text, diagrams, numerical examples, and quizzes are original instructional material written for this site. Topics follow the standard mechatronics curriculum areas used in university and professional education, extended with a 2026 systems update. This is not a reproduction of any commercial textbook.

Design and privacy

Progress uses browser localStorage key mechatronics-studio-progress on your machine only. Nothing is uploaded. Clearing site data resets progress.
