Project B · Closed loop position

Close a position loop on a DC motor or hobby servo style plant with an encoder or potentiometer. Implement discrete PID with anti windup. Capture a step response plot. This is the MIT 2.737 lab spirit and Georgia Tech motors module spirit in miniature.

Success criteria

  • Step reference produces a plotted response with measurable rise time and overshoot.
  • Output saturation is visible and anti windup is demonstrated (compare on versus off).
  • Sample rate is stated and measured.
  • Gains and supply voltage are recorded with the plot.
Suggested bill of materials MCU, motor driver or ESC appropriate to your motor, encoder or potentiometer feedback, current limited supply, optional series current sense, and a safe mechanical stop.

Procedure sketch

  1. Open loop smoke test at low voltage with current limit on.
  2. Verify feedback direction matches actuator direction (sign error is the classic first bug).
  3. Implement discrete PID from Module 9 habits: timed interrupt, anti windup, filtered derivative.
  4. Step the reference. Tune proportionally first, then add integral carefully, then derivative if noise allows.
  5. Repeat one step with anti windup disabled to show the unwind pathology, then re enable it.

Worked failure · sign error

Positive error commands the motor the wrong way. The loop runs away until the current limit or a mechanical stop saves the hardware. Fix: confirm feedback sign in open loop before enabling integral action.

Safety Secure the load. Start with low gains and low voltage. Keep fingers clear of pinch points. Prefer a current limited bench supply over a high capacity battery for first bring up.

Key terms

Step response
Output reaction to a sudden reference change.
Anti windup
Integral protection during saturation.
Sign error
Feedback or command polarity flipped; causes runaway.
Rise time
How quickly the output approaches the new reference.
