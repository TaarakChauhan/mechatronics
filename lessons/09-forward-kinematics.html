Module 9 · Robot mechanics and estimation

Forward kinematics intuition

Forward kinematics answers a practical question: given the joint variables, where is the tool, and which way does it point? You do not need a full Denavit Hartenberg derivation on day one. You need a sketch habit, a sense of serial chains, and the humility to verify poses in simulation before trusting a teach pendant number on a live cell.

Serial chains in words

A serial arm is a sequence of links joined by actuated joints. Each joint adds a degree of freedom. Starting from the base frame, you walk outward: rotate or translate by the joint value, then apply the fixed geometry of the next link. After the last joint you arrive at the tool frame.

Open texts present two common formalisms. Denavit Hartenberg parameters pack each joint and link into a compact transform chain. Product of exponentials uses twists and a home configuration. Both are worth knowing by name. Both compute the same physical pose when applied carefully.

Learning outcomes Sketch a serial chain. Explain how joint variables map to end effector pose at an intuition level. Know when DH or product of exponentials appears in open texts. Relate FK readouts to teach pendant practice.

What FK does not claim

Forward kinematics is geometry, not dynamics. It does not tell you how much torque you need. It does not guarantee a path between two poses is collision free. It does not invent missing joint sensors. It does tell you the pose that the current joint readings imply, which is the foundation for visualisation, teach points, and many inverse methods.

Studio connection

Module 4 sized actuators. Module 6 tuned loops on joints or on simple plants. Forward kinematics is the bridge when several joints cooperate to place a tool in space. Module 8 ROS TF trees become readable once you see each joint as an updating transform in the chain.

Worked scenario · two link planar arm

A planar arm has link lengths 0.3 m and 0.25 m. Joint one is 30 degrees from the base X axis. Joint two is 40 degrees relative to link one. The tool tip sits at the sum of the two link vectors. Sketching the vectors on paper recovers the same tip coordinates a simulator reports. If your sketch and the FK readout disagree, trust neither until the joint zero positions are verified.

Studio lab idea In simulation or on paper, define a two link planar arm. Sweep joint two while holding joint one fixed. Plot tip X and Y. Mark the reachable workspace ring. Compare with a Modern Robotics style software exercise if you have the tools installed.

Key terms

Forward kinematics (FK)
Mapping from joint variables to end effector pose.
Serial chain
Links connected end to end by joints from base to tool.
Denavit Hartenberg (DH)
A classical parameterisation of serial robot transforms.
Product of exponentials
A twist based alternative to DH used in Modern Robotics.
Teach pendant
Operator interface for recording and playing robot poses.
