Module 9 · Robot mechanics and estimation

Motion planning overview

Control keeps a system near a reference. Planning decides what reference should even be. In factories, CAM packages and CNC interpolators plan toolpaths. In mobile robots, planners route through maps. In arms, planners weave through joint space so links do not strike the world.

Three layers people mix up

  1. Task planning chooses goals and orderings (pick A before B).
  2. Motion planning finds a collision free geometric path or a timed trajectory.
  3. Tracking control executes the trajectory with feedback.

Mechatronics Studio Modules 4 to 6 mostly lived in layer three. Module 8 named autonomy. This lesson opens layer two.

Learning outcomes Separate path planning from trajectory timing and from low level control. Contrast grid search, sampling based planning, and optimization based ideas at a conceptual level. Explain why collision geometry and configuration space matter. Connect planners to ROS 2 navigation and manipulation stacks without claiming API mastery.

Configuration space intuition

Planning in workspace XYZ is not enough for an arm. A body may fit where a joint configuration still collides elsewhere. Configuration space lifts obstacles into the space of joint variables. That idea appears throughout Modern Robotics and MIT Manipulation notes.

Families of methods

Grid or graph search works well in low dimensions with clear costs. Sampling based methods such as PRM and RRT grow roadmaps or trees by probing random configurations. They scale to higher dimensional arms. Optimization based methods shape smooth trajectories under constraints. MIT notes also discuss Graphs of Convex Sets ideas for modern trajectory optimization. You only need the taxonomy here.

Why mechatronic constraints enter early

Motor torque limits, joint speed limits, and balance constraints turn pure geometric paths into timed trajectories. A feasible path that demands infinite acceleration is not a plan your amplifiers can run.

Worked scenario · cobot tends a CNC

A naive joint space linear move clips a vise. A planner that checks collision meshes raises the elbow, lengthens cycle time by 0.4 s, and keeps the cell safe without redesigning the fixture. The mechatronic win is often software geometry, not a heavier axis.

Studio lab idea In a 2D grid, implement BFS or A star for a differential drive footprint. Then list three reasons the same algorithm is awkward for a 6 DOF arm, pointing to sampling based methods as the usual next step.

Key terms

Configuration space
Space of joint variables where collisions can be reasoned about consistently.
RRT / PRM
Sampling based planners that probe random configurations.
Path vs trajectory
Geometry alone versus geometry with timing.
Collision mesh
Geometric model used to test whether links strike the world.
Task planning
Choosing goals and orderings above motion planning.
